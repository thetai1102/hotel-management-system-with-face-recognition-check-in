About the Hotel

A modern hotel system for a smoother guest journey.

Maison Lumière is a demonstration hotel website created for the Hotel Management System with Face Recognition Check-in project.

Hospitality supported by technology.

The system supports the hotel workflow from guest registration and Customer Portal booking requests to receptionist check-in, face verification, check-out, payment recording, invoice information, and reporting.

During check-in, the face verification feature compares the guest's stored reference image with a current face image provided at the hotel. It is designed as a supporting verification tool and does not replace receptionist judgment.

The current demonstration performs face verification between two images. Liveness and anti-spoofing detection are outside the scope of the current implementation.

Booking

Customer Portal Booking

Guests can register an account, maintain profile information, browse rooms, and submit booking requests through the Customer Portal.

Verification

Face Verification Support

Receptionists can compare a stored reference face image with the guest's current face image to support identity verification during check-in.

Management

Staff Dashboard

Authorized staff can manage rooms, customers, bookings, face check-in, check-out, recorded payments, and operational reports.
