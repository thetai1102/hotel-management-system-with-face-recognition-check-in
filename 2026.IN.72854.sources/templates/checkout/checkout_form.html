Checkout Process
  1. Select a booking that has already been checked in.
  2. Select the payment method.
  3. Record the payment result.
  4. Every payment attempt is stored in the database.
  5. If payment is Paid, the booking becomes Checked out.
  6. The room becomes Available after successful checkout.
  7. The system creates a checkout record and invoice.
