How it works
  1. Select an online booking that has already completed Face Check-in.
  2. Select the payment method.
  3. Select the payment result: Paid, Unpaid, or Failed.
  4. The payment attempt is stored permanently in the database.
  5. If payment is Paid, the booking becomes Checked out.
  6. After successful checkout, the room becomes Available.
  7. The system creates an Online Checkout record and displays the invoice.
